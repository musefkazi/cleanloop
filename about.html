CLEANLOOP

Smart Waste Station with Automatic Segregation

Problem

Improper waste segregation, especially mixing of wet and dry waste, can contaminate recyclable waste and create unhygienic surroundings.

Objectives

  • Detect wet and dry waste at point of disposal
  • Automatically divert wrong waste
  • Monitor fill level and weight
  • Provide user guidance and alerts
  • Maintain hygienic surroundings
  • Create a scalable low-cost solution

Technology

Moisture Sensor Ultrasonic Sensor Load Cell Servo Motor LCD Buzzer Microcontroller

Future Scope

  • IoT integration
  • AI-based waste identification
  • Solar-powered operation
  • Data analytics
  • Large-scale campus deployment
