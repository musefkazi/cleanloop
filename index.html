How it works

Automatic segregation in five simple steps

01

User throws waste

Waste is deposited into the automated disposal station.

02

Moisture detection

The sensor measures moisture content to identify wet or dry material.

03

Decision

The system determines whether the waste matches the correct bin category.

04

Automatic diversion

A servo motor rotates the flap to send the item to the correct receptacle.

05

Fill-level monitoring

Ultrasonic sensors and load cells continuously monitor capacity and weight.
